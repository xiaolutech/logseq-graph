# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We treat other contributors with patience and assume good faith. Technical disagreement belongs in
the code, the tests, and the measured numbers — not in remarks about the person making the argument.
Be direct about a problem, and be specific about the evidence.

Unacceptable behaviour includes personal attacks, insults, dismissive comments about someone's
experience or background, unwelcome sexual attention, harassment in any form, publishing another
person's private information without permission, and sustained disruption of discussion after a
moderator has asked for it to stop.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code review, and any project space, and to
behaviour representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's private contact channel.
Include what happened and where; do not paste the exchange into a public issue. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove or edit contributions that violate this standard, and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation after a warning, or immediately for severe
behaviour.

Maintainers are held to the same standard and may recuse themselves from handling a report in which
they are involved.
